About

I began as a theatre set designer, and over time my path has evolved to embrace the possibilities offered by new media and digital visual storytelling. Today I work as a multimedia artist, combining 3D modelling, interactive installations and research on artificial intelligence. My practice is grounded in the idea of bringing the culturally repressed back to light, using emerging technologies as tools for building collective and shared narratives. Through a critical approach to the language of code and to the technologies I employ, I try to explore the potential of digital media not only as means of representation, but as active devices of memory, transformation and imagination.
